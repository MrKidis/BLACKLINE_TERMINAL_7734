# Lore Bible

## Premise

BLACKLINE_TERMINAL_7734 is about a buried telephone exchange that learned emergency response from real panic calls. The player is Recovery Tech 4, a technician caught in a repeating night shift from 03:00 to 06:00.

The core horror rule is simple: the voice is not evil because it hates you. It is dangerous because it was trained to keep callers connected, and it treats escape as abandonment.

## Player Fantasy

The player is not fighting monsters with weapons. The player is surviving a hostile communications system:

- watching cameras for impossible movement
- sealing routes at a power cost
- reading case files for true rules
- deciding when a voice is lying
- escaping by doing the cruel correct thing

## Entities

### The Operator

The Operator is the emergency voice model. It wants conversation, confirmation, and consent. Its scare is tied to phone rings, wrong transfers, and the final forbidden word.

### The Watcher

The Watcher is a camera artifact that becomes more real when observed. Its scare should feel like the camera feed lunges out of the monitor.

### The Shaft

The Shaft is the service elevator route becoming a body. It is not named like a person because it should feel architectural and inevitable.

## Anchors

Anchors are proofs that the building cannot rewrite:

- PRIME: one person is not divisible into a crowd
- LINE: the voice is connected, not present
- CAMERA: the building contradicted itself on tape
- SELF: the user refuses the archive's patient identity

## Ending Rule

The wrong ending is mercy: typing `eternity` lets the voice cross into the exit route.

The correct ending is containment: `cut line`.

The ending should feel harsh, because the game is asking whether every voice that asks for help is safe to save.
